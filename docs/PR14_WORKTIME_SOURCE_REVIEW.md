# Bounded worktime compliance source review

2026-10-26. Synthetic engineering only. No company regime selected, policies activated, production data accessed, or payroll/payment authority granted. Tests in this batch are authored, not executed by their author.

## Official source basis

- [Labor Standards Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=N0030001), consolidated revision 2024-07-31: Articles 30, 32, 32-1, 34, 36, 86. Ordinary overtime: 46 hours/month and normal plus overtime 12 hours/day. Conditional 54/month and 138/three-month cycle requires collective agreement; employers with at least 30 workers also file. Changed rotating shifts normally require 11 hours continuous rest; the designated exception requires authority, agreement and applicable filing. Relevant 2018 amendments took effect 2018-03-01. Comp conversion follows worked overtime; it does not erase it.
- [Enforcement Rules](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=N0030002), consolidated revision 2024-03-27: Articles 17, 19, 20-1, 22, 22-1, 22-3. Combine cross-midnight normal work and work across the same employer's locations. Ordinary overtime exceeds 8/day or 40/week; actual rest-day work counts. Three-month cycles follow agreed calendar bounds. Employer headcount includes branches; applicable filing normally occurs no later than the preceding day. Standard weekly rest ordinarily prohibits more than six consecutive workdays.
- [MOL worktime definition](https://www.mol.gov.tw/1607/28162/28166/28218/28226/30382/), published 2025-09-05: controlled waiting is work; only unrestricted rest is excluded. No automatic one-hour break.
- [MOL 2023 handbook](https://www.mol.gov.tw/media/wqclbnyx/112%E5%B9%B4%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%82%E5%88%B6%E5%BA%A6%E5%8F%8A%E5%BD%88%E6%80%A7%E6%8E%AA%E6%96%BD%E6%89%8B%E5%86%8A-2023.pdf?mediaDL=true): rotating-shift rest applies when a rotating worker changes shifts, not to every pair of ordinary shifts. Flexible 2/4/8-week regimes require separate applicable designation and collective consent; their formulas are deliberately unsupported here.
- [New Taipei official Q&A](https://ilabor-ws.ntpc.gov.tw/002/ntpc/3/relfile/141/75/d5e3b1a8-a69a-4fbf-9904-da71119040b5.pdf): ordinary holiday work through eight hours is excluded from monthly overtime; excess counts. This does not authorize regular-holiday attendance.
- [OSHA 2025 overview, page 24](https://www.osha.gov.tw/media/ycjf3zp3/114%E5%B9%B4%E5%8B%9E%E5%8B%95%E5%9F%BA%E6%BA%96%E6%B3%95%E6%A6%82%E8%BF%B0.pdf): agreed three-month cycles must not arbitrarily move, overlap, or skip months.

## Pure input/output contract

`evaluateWorktimeCompliance(input)` in `utils/payroll/worktimeCompliance.ts` accepts unknown input and fails closed. It returns per-check `within-checked-limits`, `exceeded`, or `needs-review`; aggregate status prioritizes unknown prerequisites while retaining individual exceeded results. Every result retains `formalEligibility: BLOCKED`, `applicationStatus: not-applied`, `paymentStatus: not-paid`.

Supported checks: daily normal 480 minutes, weekly normal 2400, daily actual total 720, monthly overtime 2760 or authorized 3240, agreed cycle 8280 (and each cycle month 3240), standard weekly regular-holiday/rest-day designations and actual consecutive work, changed rotating shifts 660 or authorized 480 minutes. Overtime classification uses verified source minutes; monetary multipliers and compensatory-leave balances never enter these totals.

Required inputs:

- Explicit employee, month, immutable snapshot reference, `standard-8-40`, Sunday week anchor (existing approved project decision), `exception: none`, and `overtimeMode: 46 | 54-138`. Missing or unsupported regimes/exceptions are not silently replaced.
- Effective, source-bound authority records: regime, collective overtime, individual overtime, rotation applicability; additional records for the agreed 54 cycle and 8-hour rotation exception. Consent body must match explicit union existence. Proposer and reviewer must differ; reviewer cannot be subject. The source-bound `authorityOccurredAt` records actual consent/designation timing and must precede implementation; `reviewedAt` records independent evidence verification and may occur later, but never before the underlying event. Missing actual timing remains unknown. A later review does not create retrospective consent or alter future-only payroll-master protection. Evidence must cover the entire required history.
- Positive employer-wide headcount. For 54/8-hour exceptions with headcount >=30, separate timely filing evidence. Eight-hour authority includes an applicable designation reference; 54 authority includes exact cycle bounds.
- Source-bound complete daily inventory, including explicitly recorded zero-work days, approved day classifications, shift identity and verified actual intervals. Every calendar date in declared coverage must be present exactly once. Required coverage includes the selected month, full intersecting Sunday weeks, and six preceding calendar days. For 54 mode this expands to the full cycle, its enclosing Sunday weeks, and six days before those weeks. All supplied earlier days are used when counting consecutive work. The authority period must cover the entire supplied inventory, and actual interval ends must remain inside that coverage.
- Each minute interval has explicit offset, whole-minute timestamps, source references and classification: normal/overtime, rest-day, or holiday-normal/holiday-overtime. Intervals cannot overlap. Each interval's start must match its Taipei work date. Cross-midnight intervals stay attached to that date. Multi-shift-day or different legal work-date interpretations need an adapter/design decision, not guessed identities.

**Trust boundary:** reference strings are not proof of authenticity. A future trusted backend adapter must read and verify immutable authority records, full source inventories, legal applicability, actual work/rest verification, classification and snapshot binding before invoking this pure function. Browser-supplied declarations must never become such evidence directly. The independently reviewed records are engineering prerequisites, not a claim that the statute mandates this particular application workflow.

## Deliberate unsupported cases and limits

- Flexible 2/4/8-week schedules, emergency exceptions, Article 33/84-1 regimes, special protected-worker rules, disputed time or day types: `needs-review`, no automatic waiver.
- This first implementation supports agreed three-calendar-month cycles starting on day 1 only. A legitimate mid-month cycle returns `needs-review`; it is not declared unlawful and is never moved to a convenient boundary.
- Classification is an upstream evidence obligation. The checker examines daily/weekly normal classifications across the entire assessed cycle and enclosing weeks. Any normal-limit excess makes monthly/cycle overtime totals untrusted (`needs-review`, no numeric `actualMinutes`), while retaining the normal-time exceeded check; it does not silently reclassify or double-count the same minutes as both daily and weekly overtime. Holiday-normal greater than eight hours requires reclassification review. Actual overtime converted to comp is counted unchanged (inference from Articles 32-1 and Rule 20-1).
- Unsupported numeric policy flags cannot grant exception authority. Rotation is explicitly applicable or not applicable; unknown does not mean exempt. Rotating input additionally requires a source-bound `coverage.restBoundary` of kind `verified-rest`, with whole-minute `from`/`to` timestamps, snapshot and source references, ending at inventory start and proving at least 11 continuous hours without work. No absent predecessor or overnight tail is assumed. This bounded adapter does not yet accept a predecessor-only alternative; that returns needs-review. A shift ID is an evidenced rotation identity, not merely a clock-in time.
- Current-history integrity, consent authenticity, adjacent policy versions and full legal clearance require backend integration. This file changes no existing payroll calculation, workflow gate or payment path.
